About TEAM SHAURYA

TEAM SHAURYA is a student group focused on using knowledge, teamwork and technology to contribute towards rural communities.

Innovation

We explore technology and creative ideas to solve practical problems in our communities.

Community

A Community-Focused Initiative

Under our A-CEP project, TEAM SHAURYA worked with rural communities through activities focused on cleanliness, education and student support.
